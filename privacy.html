Last updated: October 16, 2026

No vendemos ni compartimos tus datos; solo se usan para publicar los videos propios de inodeAI.

Data we access

With your authorization through TikTok Login Kit, inodeAI Uploader accesses: your basic TikTok profile (open ID, display name, avatar — scope user.info.basic) and permission to upload and publish videos (video.upload, video.publish).

How it is used

This data is used only to identify the authorized account and to post the owner's own inodeAI videos to that account. Access tokens are stored locally on the owner's machine and are not transmitted elsewhere.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share TikTok data with any third party, and do not use it for advertising or analytics.

Retention

Tokens are kept only as long as needed to upload videos and are deleted when access is revoked.

Revoking access

You can revoke access at any time in the TikTok app: Profile → Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Apps and services permissions → remove inodeAI Uploader.
